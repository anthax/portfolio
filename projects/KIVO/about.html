OUR STORY

Nigerian food. A different perspective.

Kivo was created to celebrate Nigerian cuisine through a modern lens. We use local ingredients, time-honoured flavours, and thoughtful techniques to create food that brings people together.

PHILOSOPHY

Ingredients

We source locally and let quality ingredients speak for themselves.

Technique

Traditional Nigerian flavours meet modern cooking techniques.

Experience

From the plate to the table, every detail is designed to make you feel at home.

CHEF

Amara Okafor

Chef Amara Okafor brings together Nigerian culinary traditions and modern techniques. Her cooking is rooted in simplicity, seasonality, and a deep respect for local ingredients.

Cooking Philosophy

“Good food should honour where it comes from and bring people closer together.”
